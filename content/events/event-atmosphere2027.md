**Open science on the open web: research tools, communities, infrastructure and institutions, on AT Protocol and beyond.**

This year ATScience teams up with the Institute of Open Science Practices (IOSP) for two days on the tools, communities, infrastructure and institutions shaping the future of open science, and the gaps we still need to fill together. It is a side event to <a href="https://atmosphereconf.org" target="_blank">AtmosphereConf 2027</a> in the Netherlands.

Think modular research, continuous publishing, collective intelligence, citizen science, trust and attribution, research data, AI that researchers can run and shape together, and new models for funding and governing research, whether on AT Protocol or adjacent to it.

### Event Info
**April 29–30, 2027**
The Netherlands (venue to be announced)

## Call for proposals

Doing research, building for researchers, or rethinking how knowledge gets made, shared, discovered and trusted? A tool you built, or a whole new institution? Bring a talk, run a workshop, or show your project at the demo bazaar.

Proposals are submitted through the <a href="https://cfp.atmosphereconf.org/atmoco2027/cfp" target="_blank">AtmosphereConf 2027 call for proposals</a>. Choose the **ATScience** track.

- **Round 1:** closes at the end of November 2026. Submit here if you need visa or travel support.
- **Round 2:** mid-January to mid-February 2027.
