Projects

Discover innovative tools and platforms built on ATProto for the scientific community. From social knowledge networks and research feeds to AI-powered discovery tools and note-taking integrations, these projects are transforming how researchers share, discover, and collaborate on scientific work in the decentralized web.

Browse Projects

  1. Octosphere

    Automatically syncs research publications from octopus.ac to ATProto

  2. Layers

    Linguistic annotation on ATProto

  3. Paperstars

    Paperstars is a platform where researchers can rate and review published scientific papers

  4. Matadisco

    An open, decentralized network for data discovery on AT Protocol

  5. Open Community Notes

    Draft specifications for an open and interoperable Community Notes protocol by Johnathan Warden

  6. nebra

    Stream scientific data live on the ATProtocol, with a simple, fast Python module.

  7. Observ.ing

    A decentralized platform for biodiversity observations (like iNaturalist on AT Protocol)

  8. lexicons.bio

    AT Protocol record schemas for biodiversity observations

  9. science.latha.org

    A permissioned appview for scientific documents on AT Protocol

  10. GreenEarth Social

    Advanced open source algorithms for social media on ATProto
